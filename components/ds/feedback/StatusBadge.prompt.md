Locked-vocabulary badge for stock status — the four labels ("en stock" / "faible" / "rupture" / "non renseigné") and their colors are fixed by DESIGN.md and must never be paraphrased or recolored per-screen.

```jsx
<StatusBadge status="rupture" />
```

Critical rule: `rupture` (danger/red) and `non renseigné` (neutral/gray) must always be visually distinct — they are different severities.
